Introduction

A gentle place to begin.

Every life has a story worth honoring. Every family deserves kindness and respect.

End of life can bring many questions and emotions. You may be thinking ahead for yourself, caring for someone you love, or simply trying to understand what comes next. Whatever brought you here, you deserve the time and space to be heard.

Appalachian Way Home offers compassionate, non-medical end-of-life guidance and companionship. My hope is to provide a steady presence for individuals and families as they move through this part of life. That may mean listening to what is on your heart, helping make room for important conversations, creating meaningful moments, offering comfort, or simply being present when words are no longer needed.

I believe death is a natural part of life, and the way we approach it deserves tenderness, dignity, honesty, and respect. There is no single right way to experience this journey. Each person has their own wishes, each family has its own history, and those differences matter.

No one should have to feel alone in facing the end of life. My hope is to walk alongside you and your loved ones with patience, understanding, and care, honoring what matters most to you.

About Me

My roots are here in these mountains.

Western North Carolina · Eight generations · One home

Some things are rooted deeper than words can express. For me, those roots run through the mountains of Western North Carolina, where my family's lineage stretches back eight generations.

These mountains have always represented something special to me: a sense of belonging, a connection to those who came before us, and the quiet understanding that home is more than a place. It is where our stories begin, where our memories live, and where we are held by the things that matter most.

That deep connection to family, community, and the generations before me is what inspired me to create Appalachian Way Home. I have learned how much it means for a person to feel remembered, respected, and surrounded by care. I want to carry those values into the support I offer others.

As an end-of-life doula, I hope to be a caring, steady presence for people and families during one of life's most tender times. Sometimes there are stories to share or wishes to talk through. Sometimes the most meaningful thing I can offer is to sit beside someone and simply listen.

I would be honored to walk alongside you and your loved ones with an open heart, a gentle presence, and deep respect for the journey you are on.

With gratitude and an open heart,
Alisha Jamerson

Compassionate & Practical Support

What I Offer

Every person's journey is unique, and the support they need may change along the way. My goal is to offer compassionate, practical assistance tailored to you and your loved ones.

Ways I Can Help

  • Practical Help & Everyday Support
  • Physical Comfort & Companionship
  • Advance Care Planning & Guidance
  • Resources & Referrals
  • Family & Caregiver Support
  • End-of-Life Support

These are just a few examples to help start the conversation. We can walk this journey together, meeting you where you are and finding the kind of support that best fits your individual needs.

End-of-life doula services are non-medical and are meant to complement, not replace, professional medical or hospice care.

What to Expect

We can start with a conversation.

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out. You can share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with. We can talk about your questions, what feels important right now, and whether my support would be helpful to you or your family.

There is no pressure to find the right words. We can begin wherever you are.
